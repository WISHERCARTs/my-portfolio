# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
- **Tech Recruiters & HR:** Tech recruiters and HR personnel looking for skilled Data & AI developers or software engineering interns.
- **Potential Clients:** Potential employers/clients looking for freelance or contract services.
- **Professors & Peers:** Faculty and students from Mahidol University DST program or the broader ICT community checking out academic projects.

## Product Purpose
- To showcase Wish Nakthong's programming capabilities, education, skills, certificates, and projects as a 2nd-year B.Sc. in Digital Science & Technology (DST) student.
- To demonstrate technical competency through interactive elements (e.g. streaming Gemini chatbot widget).
- To make a memorable impression through professional, modern, and highly accessible design.

## Brand Personality
- **Innovative & Tech-Forward:** Uses clean layouts, responsive micro-interactions, and visual touches suitable for the Data Science & AI domains.
- **Professional & Trustworthy:** Strong typography, clear hierarchy, high-contrast, accessible text.
- **Engaging & Human:** Feels alive, responsive, and friendly through subtle hover states and motion.

## Anti-references
- Saturated neon gradients or neon text that are hard to read and look unprofessional.
- Extremely rounded borders (e.g. `rounded-[32px]`) on content sections or cards.
- Identical card grids with no visual hierarchy or distinction.

## Design Principles
- **Clarity over Clutter:** Strong focus on readable content and spacing. Spacing should guide the eye and allow scanning.
- **Intentional Motion:** Smooth Framer Motion transitions that serve a purpose (always respecting user reduced-motion preferences).
- **Showcase Technical Competency:** High-quality, pixel-perfect layouts, responsive breakpoints, and fully functioning components.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG 2.1 AA level compliance (high-contrast text, clear font scaling, clean semantic elements).
- Perfect responsiveness across mobile, tablet, and desktop viewports.
